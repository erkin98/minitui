A failed merge starts a repair turn only when one can help. No turn starts after the run was cancelled, before an app is on screen, for the host's own refusal of a repaired spec, while another repair is running, or after three repairs in one app session; each skip is logged with its reason. Each repair's first prompt quotes the spec that is on screen, and its retries quote its own last reply. A merge that settles now shows its progress bar at 100%.
